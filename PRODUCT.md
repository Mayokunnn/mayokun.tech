# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, potential clients, and fellow developers evaluating Mayokun Areola as a full-stack developer (frontend-focused). They land here from a resume, LinkedIn, or GitHub link, usually skimming quickly to judge credibility and craft before deciding whether to reach out or read further.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that establishes Mayokun as a skilled, detail-oriented full-stack/frontend developer. It showcases projects, experience, and a way to get in touch. Success looks like a visitor forming a strong first impression of craft within seconds, then following through to view work or make contact.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, confident, modern. The site itself should double as proof of frontend skill — precise typography, considered motion, and a distinct visual point of view rather than a neutral, safe template. Confident without being loud; the craft should read as intentional, not decorative.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS/template portfolio look: flat monochrome gray palette with no accent color, centered hero + card-grid layout that could belong to anyone, default system-font feel, no motion personality. Avoid anything that reads as an unmodified template.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell — the site's own polish is the strongest evidence of frontend skill.
- Confident restraint — commit to a distinct point of view, but don't sacrifice legibility or usability for flair.
- Every screen should have a clear focal point; avoid uniform, undifferentiated sections.
- Motion is deliberate and purposeful, never decorative filler.
- Fast and lightweight — a portfolio that's slow to load undercuts its own pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum across light and dark themes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Full keyboard navigation for menu, links, and contact form.
